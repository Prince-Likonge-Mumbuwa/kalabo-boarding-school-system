# Free reference images for the Biology diagrams

Our diagrams are drawn by code (SVG), never pasted in. These sources are
references for getting shapes, positions and part names right. Each licence
was checked on the file's own page in November 2026. Check it again before
copying anything.

## How each licence may be used

| Licence | What we may do |
|---|---|
| **Public domain / CC0** (LadyofHats, Gray's 1918, Sobotta 1909, US Government: NCI SEER, NIAID, NIH) | Anything: trace the outlines, reuse paths, adapt freely. No credit needed (we give it anyway). |
| **CC BY** (OpenStax, Servier Medical Art, the Chittka & Brockmann ear, Lynch) | May adapt or redraw closely, but the drawing must carry a credit line (as we do for the map data). |
| **CC BY-SA / GFDL** (Zephyris leaf, Wapcaplet heart, the annotated eye) | Look only. Do not trace or copy paths. A close copy would have to be shared under the same licence. |
| Copyrighted books (the Rohen atlas, past-paper booklets, textbooks) | Look only, for facts and conventions. |
